    How to use MedLY with Live Caption

    1. Turn on Live Caption (Ctrl + Windows + L).
    2. Click the New session (Start) button and begin the conversation.
    3. When the conversation is finished, click the Stop button to end the recording.

    Note

    If Live Caption appears in two lines, the text may not be recognized properly!
    Please adjust the size of the Live Caption window so that the transcription appears in a single line.
